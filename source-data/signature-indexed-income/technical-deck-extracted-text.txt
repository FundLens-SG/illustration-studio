The Policy Value is calculated as follows:
1. On the Issue Date
• Equivalent to the Net Premium (i.e. first premium paid less applicable Premium Charge) less Monthly 
Deductions for the first Policy Month.
2.   On each Policy Monthiversary (MVY)
a. any Withdrawal made or income payout since the last MVY; 
b. any Monthly Deductions for the following Policy Month; 
c. any Pro-Rated Surrender Charge , any pro-rata Unvested 
Policy Value Booster and/or refund of premium due to limits 
on maximum premium; and
d. any TI claim paid since the last MVY .
• Equals to adding up:
(a) Policy Value as of the last MVY; 
(b) any Net Premiums received since the last 
MVY; and
(c) Interest and Policy Value Booster earned for 
that Policy Month.
