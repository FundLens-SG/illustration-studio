        Important risks & disclosures

        SLH (Signature Legacy Harvest) is a participating whole-life policy underwritten by Manulife (Singapore) Pte. Ltd.. This illustration projects to policy year 120 using the insurer's Reinvested bonus schedule — non-guaranteed terminal bonus stays inside the policy and compounds the surrender value. The Adhoc 50% drawdown column in the table is informational: it shows how much cash a policyholder could pull from the accumulated non-guaranteed bonus at that policy year.

        Bonuses, dividends, and the surrender values shown are not guaranteed until declared. The guaranteed surrender benefit may be less than total premiums paid, particularly in the early policy years.

        Key risks of a participating plan — your premiums are pooled with other policyholders' and invested in Manulife's Participating Fund. As a policyholder you share in the fund's investment risk (lower-than-expected returns), insurance risk (higher-than-expected claims), and expense risk (higher-than-expected fund management costs). Any of these can reduce the bonuses or dividends you receive.

        USD values are converted to SGD at the live exchange rate when comparing with SGD-denominated InvestReady plans.

        For the official figures and complete terms, refer to the Manulife Policy Illustration, Product Summary, and Bundled Product Disclosure Document. In any discrepancy, those documents prevail over this advisor working tool.

        Illustrated at 7.50% p.a. BR — the higher of two Illustrated Investment Rates of Return on the official Policy Illustration. The lower rate (5.25% p.a.) and the actual realised bonus rate may differ materially from this projection.
