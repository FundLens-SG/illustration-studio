        • Non-guaranteed projections. The 4.25% Illustrated Investment Rate of Return drives both the monthly coupon and the cash value shown here. The lower 3.00% scenario (also in the Policy Illustration) is not shown. Actual bonus rates / coupon payouts may be materially lower than illustrated and are only declared annually by the insurer.
        • Coupon income. Monthly coupon (annualised 3.60% of single premium at 4.25% IRR) begins from policy month 37 and continues to maturity at age 120. Approximately 1.24% is the guaranteed portion; the remaining ~2.36% is non-guaranteed and depends on Participating Fund performance.
        • Death & Terminal Illness Benefit. The death benefit floor is 105% of single premium, plus accumulated non-guaranteed bonuses. The "umbrella" framing in this illustration represents the guaranteed insurance coverage element of a participating product — payable to beneficiaries regardless of income drawn during the policy term.
        • Surrender value. Early surrender values are below total premium paid and may be zero in policy day 1. Where a guaranteed-surrender-value floor benefit applies, total surrender value at the floor age is guaranteed at a percentage of single premium (e.g. 80%) as set out in the policy contract. Final surrender value is non-guaranteed and depends on the Participating Fund.
        • Premium financing risks. Where premium financing is applied (default 72% bank co-share at 2.00% p.a. interest, interest-only) the customer is responsible for ongoing loan interest for the life of the policy. Interest rate risk: rates may rise above the illustrated level, reducing or eliminating net coupon income. Refinancing risk: the loan facility may not be renewed at terms equivalent to those quoted. Margin / repayment risk: the loan principal is repaid from policy proceeds at surrender or maturity; if policy value is less than the loan outstanding, the customer is responsible for the shortfall.
        • Currency & tax. Premium and benefits are denominated in Singapore Dollars. No tax advice is given; clients should consult their own tax advisor on local tax treatment of life insurance proceeds and premium financing interest.

        Important risks & disclosures

        SLH models a participating whole-life variant in this tool. The factor tables used are illustrative and may not reflect any specific insurer's current schedule. This projection runs to policy year 120 using a Reinvested bonus schedule — non-guaranteed terminal bonus stays inside the policy and compounds the surrender value. The Adhoc 50% drawdown column in the table is informational: it shows how much cash a policyholder could pull from the accumulated non-guaranteed bonus at that policy year.

        Bonuses, dividends, and the surrender values shown are not guaranteed until declared. The guaranteed surrender benefit may be less than total premiums paid, particularly in the early policy years.

        Key risks of a participating plan — your premiums are pooled with other policyholders' and invested in the insurer's Participating Fund. As a policyholder you share in the fund's investment risk (lower-than-expected returns), insurance risk (higher-than-expected claims), and expense risk (higher-than-expected fund management costs). Any of these can reduce the bonuses or dividends you receive.

        USD values are converted to SGD at the live exchange rate when comparing with SGD-denominated ILP variants in this tool.
